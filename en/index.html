Is PDF compression or a standalone general-purpose OCR tool available?

Available tools include PDF merge and split, images to PDF, PDF to JPG, image optimization, QR generation, JSON formatting, text statistics, and everyday calculations. A standalone general-purpose OCR tool and PDF compression are not available. The Local Proof of Address Demo provides limited in-browser text extraction and English or Simplified Chinese OCR for scans; it does not connect to third-party verification.
