Company Seal & Stamp Editor

Create seal artwork using a full company name or personal name. Choose a circle, ellipse, or square layout and a regional style preset; edit curved main text, inner and bottom text, a center symbol or logo, and two heading lines. Fine-tune fonts, size, spacing, lines, color, and texture, then export a transparent PNG or paper-effect image.

No sign-up requiredProcessed locally in your browserWorks on mobile and desktop
HOW IT WORKS

How to use

  1. Choose company or personal mode and a preset, then enter the company name or personal name and seal text.
  2. Set the shape, color, borders, and texture, and edit the font, placement, and layout of each text region; the preview updates as you edit.
  3. Download a transparent PNG or create a paper-effect image; settings can be saved in this browser.
FAQ

About Company Seal & Stamp Editor

How long can a company name be?

Company mode supports full names up to 80 grapheme clusters; personal mode allows up to 8 grapheme clusters.

Which seal elements can I edit?

Edit curved main text, inner-ring text, bottom text, a center symbol or logo, and two heading lines; adjust shape, color, lines, fonts, spacing, size, placement, and texture.

Is seal artwork uploaded?

No. Seal artwork is created in this browser and settings are saved locally; the page does not need to send your input to ChainSeek.

Which export backgrounds are available?

Transparent PNGs are ready for further layout; paper-effect exports are opaque PNGs.

Does this tool guarantee legal validity or usage rights?

No. This tool is for artwork editing only. Verify your own asset and font licenses, and check applicable rules for seal use, authorization, and legal requirements.
