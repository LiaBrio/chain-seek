Local Proof of Address Demo & Official Bill Samples

First inspect complete bill images published by Con Edison and NYC DEP, then optionally use a fully local file demo to compare extracted name, address, and date candidates. The samples are not a private bill for any user; local cues are not official verification and do not connect to issuers or third-party verification services.

No sign-up requiredProcessed locally in your browserWorks on mobile and desktop
HOW IT WORKS

How to use

  1. Browse each page of the two issuers' complete bill samples and locate account details, billing period, amount due, usage or readings, itemized charges, and payment sections.
  2. Click an image to open and zoom the original, then compare it with the official bill guide. Sample information does not represent anyone's current account.
  3. Optionally select a PDF, image, or plain-text file; the browser extracts text locally and compares it with a reference name, address, and age limit. Scanned-page OCR supports English or Simplified Chinese.
  4. Verify real bills through an official issuer channel you locate independently. This demo does not query external records or decide authenticity.
FAQ

About Local Proof of Address Demo & Official Bill Samples

Are these bills currently used by real customers?

No. These are complete bill samples published by the issuers, with sample information to help explain the fields. They are not any user's private bill or current account.

Are the official sample images modified?

No. All four public Con Edison and NYC DEP sample pages are shown unchanged—not redrawn, cropped, or annotated. The page links to both issuers' bill guides.

Can layout alone establish whether a bill is genuine?

No. Real bills can vary in layout and fields, and a forged document can imitate an appearance. To verify an actual record, use an official channel you locate independently.

Does the local demo provide an official verification decision?

No. It only extracts readable text in this browser, compares strings with the reference values you provide, and shows basic file cues. It does not query issuers, banks, governments, credit agencies, or third-party registries, or decide authenticity or residency eligibility.

Are my files uploaded or saved?

No. Selected files and reference information are processed only in this browser's memory; they are not sent to a verification API or saved by ChainSeek. The OCR engine and language models load on demand from same-origin ChainSeek assets; the app does not store OCR language models in browser storage. Only process files you are authorized to view.

Which file types and languages are supported?

PDF, PNG, JPEG, WebP, and plain text are supported; files are limited to 15 MB, plain text to 2 MB, and PDFs to 8 pages. PDF text layers are read directly; images and scanned pages can use English or Simplified Chinese OCR locally. Review the original manually if extraction is incomplete.
