HOW IT WORKS

How to use

  1. Choose a demo region and fictional category (electricity, water, gas, or internet); these only change date/currency display and generic units, not any real provider template.
  2. Edit fictional issuer, customer, address, billing period, and amount fields, or click “Fill fictional sample”; the preview updates immediately and always keeps the DEMO / NOT A BILL mark.
  3. Download or share the demo PDF. Each page embeds a visible mark in a flattened page image; the page has no mark-removal option.
FAQ

About Fictional Utility Bill Template & Live Preview

Does this read or verify a real bill?

No. It only creates a fictional layout with a permanent demo mark. It reads no files, connects to no issuer or third party, and does not verify accounts, addresses, or residency eligibility.

How do regions and categories work?

Regions only change date formatting and the default currency; categories only switch generic units for electricity (kWh), water/gas (m³), or internet (GB). There is no regional provider directory, real bill template, bank statement, or issuer branding.

Which fields can I edit?

You can edit fictional issuer and customer names, address, reference, currency, dates, service period, usage, fees, tax, previous balance, payments, and a note; you can also switch demo regions and generic utility categories. The sample button restores the general fictional example.

Can I download or share a PDF?

Yes. The PDF is generated locally in your browser. Supported devices open the system share sheet; otherwise the file downloads. Every page embeds the DEMO / NOT A BILL mark directly as an image, with additional notices at the top and bottom.

Can I turn off or pay to remove the mark?

No. The preview and export always include the demo mark; there is no option to hide it, turn it off, or pay to remove it.

Are my inputs uploaded or saved?

No. Fields, preview, and PDF are processed locally in this browser and are not uploaded or saved. Use fictional details only; the output is not a bill, payment request, proof of address, or official record.
