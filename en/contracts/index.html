HOW IT WORKS

How to use

  1. Choose a jurisdiction and one of seven document types. The first version of the employment separation certificate is for Mainland China only.
  2. Enter key terms and update the draft; edit the text directly in the paper preview.
  3. Add or drop in a seal image, then move and resize it on the preview. Choose A4, A5, Letter, Legal, or custom dimensions; select a paper texture and color; handwrite a signature; then download the PDF, open the system share sheet, or download a Word-compatible draft.
FAQ

About Contract Templates & Preview

Which contract types are included?

The first set includes NDAs, services/consulting, sale or supply of goods, independent contractors, software/IP licences, distribution/agency, and a Mainland China employment separation certificate.

Which regions are covered?

Drafting guidance is provided for Mainland China, Hong Kong, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, the United States, the United Kingdom (England and Wales sample by default), and EU cross-border matters. The EU is not one contract-law jurisdiction; a U.S. state law must also be selected.

Can I change the paper and handwrite a signature?

Yes. Choose A4, A5, Letter, Legal, or custom dimensions, then select a paper texture and color. The signature area at the bottom supports writing with a mouse, stylus, or finger.

How do I export or share the finished document?

Select Export PDF to generate and download the finished document. Share PDF opens the system share sheet; if file sharing is unsupported, the PDF downloads instead. The Word-compatible .doc draft is also available.

Is an employment separation certificate a contract?

It is generally an employer-issued certificate, not a mutual separation agreement, settlement, or waiver. The first version is for Mainland China only.

Are contract text or seal images uploaded?

No. Form fields, text, handwritten signatures, imported seal images, and previews are processed in your browser and are not uploaded or saved by ChainSeek.

Does the seal preview count as signing or using an official seal?

No. The seal is only an image overlay for layout preview. It is not an electronic signature and does not establish authorization or legal effect.
